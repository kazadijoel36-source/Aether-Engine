Privacy Logic

Protocol Version 1.0.4 • Last Modified: May 2026

At AETHER, we operate under a Zero-Retention Framework. Your data is matter in transit, not a resource to be harvested. This policy outlines how we handle data during the transcoding process.

1. Data Neutrality

AETHER does not store, copy, or distribute the files you upload. Processing occurs in high-speed volatile memory (RAM). Once the transmutation is complete and the download session expires, the data is purged from our infrastructure via a secure after_this_request protocol.

2. Cookies and Tracking

We use essential session cookies to facilitate the "Success Redirect" and file extraction. Additionally, we use third-party services like Google AdSense which may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Ad Settings.

3. Log Data

Like all web applications, our servers collect basic log information (IP addresses, browser type). This is used strictly for security monitoring and DDOS prevention to maintain Aether's core stability.

4. Third-Party Links

Our "Fuel the Engine" donation portal links to external providers (e.g., Buy Me a Coffee). We do not control their privacy protocols and recommend reviewing their statements independently.
